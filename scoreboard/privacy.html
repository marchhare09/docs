Privacy Policy for Volleyball Scoreboard

Effective Date: 6 May 2026

March Hare Machinations ("we," "us," or "our") built the VistaScore Scoreboard app ("the App") as a tool for scorekeeping on iPhone and Apple Watch. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Information We Collect

App Preferences

The App stores your game settings locally on your iPhone, including points to win, win-by-two preference, score cap, team colors, celebration style, and display preferences. These settings are stored using Apple's UserDefaults framework within the App's private sandbox.

Purchase Information

If you leave a tip through the App's tip jar, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or billing details.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any personal identifying information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your physical location or GPS data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Any information from other apps on your device

Data Storage

Game scores are held in memory only and are not persisted between sessions. Your game settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults framework. No data is stored on external servers.

Apple Watch Communication

The App communicates with the Apple Watch companion app using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This communication occurs directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi. No data is sent to external servers.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services. The only external service used is:

  • Apple StoreKit — for optional tip jar purchase processing

This is operated by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since the App collects no personal information, it is suitable for users of all ages.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
